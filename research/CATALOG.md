# Reference catalog: popular mobile arcade mechanics

Purpose: choose **mechanic families** for original Yantrasobi cartridges. We copy no code,
assets, names, characters, art, sound, or distinctive levels. Each cartridge only borrows a
genre-level mechanic, which is not protectable expression, and uses its own name, rules
tuning, and procedural visuals.

## Method and limits (2026-11-04)
* Direct fetches of `play.google.com`, `voodoo.io`, and `habby.com` **failed**: the hosts don't
  resolve in this sandbox's network policy. Google Play listings could not be opened, so
  install counts and ratings below are **not verified against Google Play**.
* Evidence comes from web-search results (secondary sources: Wikipedia, PocketGamer.biz,
  publisher-labelled store listings in search snippets). The Evidence column names the source.
  A mechanic summary with no source is marked *(known mechanic, unverified)*.
* Priority = (how distinct the mechanic is from what we have) × (fun on one-thumb portrait) ×
  (build cost within this session).
